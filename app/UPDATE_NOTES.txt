# 0.3.6
Couch Play: the game now sits inside Game Night's window, right in the lobby's box. No more flicker, and it no longer covers the seat list
Game Night opens full screen, like a console. Press F11 to switch to a window and back
Press Y for full screen in a lobby: the game fills the whole screen
Lobby controls while you play: hold both sticks in (L3 + R3) for a second. VIEW + MENU clashed with Steam and Windows shortcuts
To quit a game on your own: press both sticks in (L3 + R3), or Esc
