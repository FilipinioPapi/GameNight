# 0.3.5
New: Couch Play online. Host an NES or SNES game, or join a friend's with a 6-letter code
Up to 4 players and 4 watchers, and you can switch between playing and watching any time
The game plays live in the lobby. Press Y for full screen
While you play, hold VIEW + MENU for a second to open lobby controls
If someone drops out, the game keeps going and their seat is held for 2 minutes
Nothing is listed publicly. Only people in Jayden's Discord can see open lobbies
