# 0.3.7
New: Game Night's own game menu. Press the Xbox or PS button in any game (or hold both sticks in, L3 + R3)
On your own it pauses the game: resume, save, load, restart, change the look, or quit to the Library (it asks first)
In Couch Play your friends keep playing while your menu is open: back to the lobby, full screen, refresh game, play or watch, leave
Pause for everyone: anyone playing can pause the game for the whole lobby, and everyone sees who paused it
Refresh game: if your controller stops working in a game, it saves your spot, restarts the game and puts you right back where you were
RetroArch's own menus are gone for good: everything is in Game Night's menu. No controller button combo quits a game any more (Esc still does)
Games now start cleanly, with none of the emulator's pop-up messages (loading, controller connected, player joined)
New looks for your games: three lighter old-TV (CRT) looks and four smoothing looks, chosen per console in Settings. Each PC uses its own look, so friends never go out of sync
A bezel around your games: a dark TV frame by default, or a console photo or a controller that lights up (Settings, per console), with sharp whole-pixel scaling. Bezels show in Couch Play too
Library: every cover shows whole, in its real box shape per console
Safer online: Game Night now stops other computers on your network from reaching the game player. Windows asks for permission once while installing this update
